Cube MTG Analyzer

Explore, build, and evaluate Magic: The Gathering cubes with card search, saved analytical queries, and booster-level statistics.

What is a cube?

A cube is a curated card pool designed for repeatable limited play. Instead of opening sealed booster packs, players draft from a hand-built environment where every card, archetype, and color balance is intentional.

What this project does

The app combines a Scryfall-powered card database with cube management tools. It helps compare a cube against real set benchmarks, estimate how often key effects appear in a booster, and document public cube lists for visitors.

Public portfolio

Public cubes are available without signing in, including their card lists and statistics. Private cube editing, custom user queries, and card additions remain available after login.
